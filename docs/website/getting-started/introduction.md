# Introduction

**Included in Free.** WB Reviews has no paid version.

WB Reviews lets visitors rate your articles and pages with stars and write a short review. You approve each review before it shows.

## What you can do

- Choose which kinds of page can be reviewed, such as posts or pages.
- Let anyone review, or only signed-in members.
- Hold every review until you approve it.
- Accept stars alone. A rating without words still counts in the average.
- Show the average, a bar for each star level, and the written reviews, 10 at a time.
- Show a small rating badge on cards and lists.
- Reply once, in public, under any review.
- See what is waiting on your dashboard, and get an optional hourly email.
- Delete all the reviews a guest left, by their email address.
- Add the rating to the page for search engines, so stars can appear in search results.

## Reviews and comments

EmDash already has comments. Comments are for conversation. WB Reviews adds a star rating, one review per person per page, an average with a count, and your reply. Reviews on directory listings belong to a different plugin, Listora. WB Reviews is for articles and pages.

## How it fits together

| Part | What it does | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The plugin | Stores reviews and gives you the Waiting, Published and Settings screens | Installed from the EmDash registry |
| The site kit | Draws the reviews and the rating badge on your pages | A few files your developer copies into the site once |

EmDash plugins from the registry run in a safe sandbox and cannot add pages or styles by themselves. That is why the visible part comes as a site kit. After your developer sets it up, you manage everything from admin.
