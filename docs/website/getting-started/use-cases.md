# Use cases

**Included in Free.** WB Reviews has no paid version.

Here are common ways to use WB Reviews. Each one lists the settings to pick and what visitors then see. Your developer must first add the components to your templates. See [Posts and pages](posts-and-pages.md).

## Star ratings under articles or tutorials

Let readers rate what they read.

1. Open **Reviews**, then **Settings**, then the **Where** tab.
2. Tick **Posts**. Choose **Save**.

Under each article, visitors see the average, a bar for each star level and the **Write a review** form. A rating with no words still counts. See [Where reviews show](../features/where-reviews-show.md).

## Reviews on product, course or service pages

Use this when you sell something and want proof from buyers.

1. On the **Where** tab, tick the content type that holds those pages, by its normal name.
2. Choose **Save**.
3. Your developer adds the Reviews component to that type's template, with its real collection name.

Visitors see the same Reviews section on each of those pages. You approve each review in **Waiting** before it shows. Only published reviews count in the average.

## Members-only reviews on a membership site

Keep reviews to people who have an account.

- On the **Who can review** tab, set **Who can leave a review** to **Signed-in members only**.

Guests see "Only members can review this page." and a **Sign in** link that brings them back. Signed-in members see the form without a name field. Their review carries their account name.

## Reply to a review in public

Answer a question or thank a reviewer.

1. Open **Published**.
2. In the **Decide** menu of the review, choose **Reply**.
3. Write up to 1000 characters and choose **Save reply**.

Visitors see your answer under the review, marked **Owner reply**, with its date. You get one reply per review. To change it, choose **Reply** again. See [Waiting and Published](../features/waiting-and-published.md#reply-to-a-review).

## Show the rating on post cards

Let readers see ratings before they click.

- Your developer adds the rating badge to the card template. See [Posts and pages](posts-and-pages.md).

Each card with a rating shows the stars, the average and the count, for example 4.5 (12). A card with no rating shows nothing. The content type must be ticked on the **Where** tab.

## Hold every review until you approve it

This is on by default.

- On the **Approval** tab, keep **Approve reviews before they show** on.

New reviews wait in **Waiting**. You choose **Approve** or **Reject** from the **Decide** menu. Guest reviews always wait. Turn the switch off and reviews from signed-in members show at once.
