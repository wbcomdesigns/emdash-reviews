# Your first review

**Included in Free.** WB Reviews has no paid version.

This walks through one review from start to finish: a visitor writes it, you approve it, and it shows on the page.

## 1. Check your settings

1. In admin, open **Reviews**, then **Settings**.
2. On the **Where** tab, make sure **Posts** is ticked. Choose **Save** if you changed it.
3. On the **Who can review** tab, leave **Anyone** chosen.

## 2. Write a review

Open a post in a private window, so you are a visitor and not signed in.

1. Under **Your rating**, choose a star.
2. Type a name under **Your name**.
3. Optional: add a **Headline** and write under **Your review**.
4. Choose **Submit review**.

You see "Thanks. Your review will show once it is approved." The review does not show on the page yet.

## 3. Approve it

1. In admin, open **Reviews**, then **Waiting**. Your review is the first row, with a **Guest** note.
2. In the **Decision** column, open **Decide** and choose **Approve**.

You see "Review published."

## 4. See it on the page

Reload the post. The **Reviews** section now shows the average, a bar for each star level, and your review. Any card that carries the rating badge shows the stars, the average and the count.

## 5. Reply

1. Open **Published** and find the review.
2. Open **Decide** and choose **Reply**.
3. Write your answer and choose **Save reply**.

The reply shows under the review, marked **Owner reply**, with its date.
