# Frequently asked questions

**Included in Free.** WB Reviews has no paid version.

## Can I review directory listings with this?

No. Reviews on directory listings belong to a different plugin, Listora. WB Reviews is for articles and pages.

## How is this different from EmDash comments?

Comments are for conversation. WB Reviews adds a star rating, one review per person per page, an average with a count, a bar for each star level, your public reply, and the rating for search engines. It has no threads and no replies between visitors.

## Can guests review?

Yes, when **Who can leave a review** is set to **Anyone**, which is the default. A guest gives a name, and an email if you ask for one. A guest review always waits for your approval.

## Do reviews go live straight away?

No. Each review waits in **Waiting** until you approve it. A change to a live review also waits, and the old text stays on the page until you approve the change.

## Can someone leave stars without writing anything?

Yes. The text is optional. The stars count in the average and the bars. They do not appear in the written list.

## Can a visitor review the same page twice?

No. A visitor has one review per page, and a second one replaces the first. A guest is recognised by a scrambled form of their internet address, or by a browser code when your hosting passes no address.

## Who sees a guest's email?

Only you, in admin. It is never shown on the site and never sent out of the plugin. You can delete all the reviews tied to an email from **Published**.

## Can I reply to a review?

Yes, once per review, from **Published**. Your reply shows under the review as **Owner reply**.

## Can reviewers edit or delete their own review?

Not yet. A "My reviews" page for members is planned for a later version. You can remove any review from **Published**, and all of a guest's reviews by email.

## Do I get an email when a review arrives?

Optionally. At most one short notice an hour while reviews are waiting, with no review text and no link. It needs an address on the **Email** tab and a site that can send mail. The dashboard card always shows the waiting count.

## Can I put the reviews in the middle of an article?

Not with the registry install. EmDash does not let registry plugins add blocks inside post text. Reviews sit where your developer places them in a template.

## Does it help with search results?

The rating is added to the page for search engines. They may show stars next to your page. Whether they do is their choice.

## Does it work in dark mode and on phones?

Yes. The reviews follow your site's colours and fit a phone screen.

## Does it work with other Wbcom plugins?

Yes. Use it alone or with any of them. They share two small kit files; keep the newer copy.

## Is there a paid version?

No. Everything described in these guides is free.
