# Where reviews show

**Included in Free.** WB Reviews has no paid version.

Reviews show in two places on your site: the full **Reviews** section on a page, and a small rating badge on cards and lists. Your developer places both once. See [Install](../getting-started/install.md).

## The Reviews section

On a page that can be reviewed, visitors see, from top to bottom:

1. A heading, **Reviews**, and the summary, for example "4.5 out of 5 · 12 reviews".
2. A bar for each star level from 5 down to 1, with how many reviews gave that level.
3. **Sort reviews**, with **Newest**, **Highest rating** and **Lowest rating**. It appears once there are two written reviews.
4. The written reviews, 10 at a time. Choose **Show more** to see the next 10.
5. The **Write a review** form. See [Writing a review as a visitor](write-a-review.md).

Each review shows the stars, the name, the date, the headline and the text. Dates use your site's time zone from EmDash site settings. If you replied, your answer shows under the review, marked **Owner reply** with its date.

## Stars without words

A visitor can leave stars alone. That rating counts in the average, the review count and the bars. It does not appear in the written list. When a page has only star ratings, the list shows "No written reviews yet" and the number of ratings.

## Only your published reviews count

The average and the count use published reviews only. A review that is waiting, or that you rejected, counts for nothing. When you approve or remove a review, the average updates at once.

## The rating badge

The badge is a short line for cards and lists: the stars, the average, and the count in brackets, for example 4.5 (12). It shows nothing for a page that has no rating. A screen reader hears "rated 4.5 out of 5 from 12 reviews".

## Which pages show reviews

Reviews and the badge appear only on content types you ticked on the **Where** tab of Settings. On any other type they draw nothing, and the visitor sees no empty form. Only published pages can be reviewed. A draft cannot receive a review.

## Search engines

When a page has a rating, WB Reviews adds it to the page's hidden data for search engines (the average and the review count). Search engines may then show stars next to your page in results. You do not set anything up for this, and it is not guaranteed that a search engine will show them.
