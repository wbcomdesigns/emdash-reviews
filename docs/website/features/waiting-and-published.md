# Waiting and Published

**Included in Free.** WB Reviews has no paid version.

You manage reviews on two admin screens under **Reviews**: **Waiting** for reviews that need a decision, and **Published** for reviews that are live. Only site admins can open them. Visitors and members cannot.

## What both screens show

Each screen opens with three numbers: **Waiting**, **Published this week** and **Average rating**. Below them are a **Find** box and a table with these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Review | The headline and text, cut short after about 80 characters. "Stars only" when there are no words |
| Page | The title of the page being reviewed, or "Removed page" if it no longer exists |
| Stars | For example "4 of 5" |
| Author | The name the visitor gave |
| Note | **Guest** for a visitor without an account. **Change waiting** when someone rewrote a live review |
| Decision | A **Decide** menu |

The table shows 25 reviews at a time. Use **Next** and **Previous** to move, and the "In this list" number to see the total.

**Find** looks in the headline, the text, the name and your reply. Type a word or a name and choose **Find**. It searches the newest 500 reviews of each kind.

## Waiting

Waiting holds new reviews and changes to live reviews. When **Nothing waiting** shows, you are done.

In the **Decide** menu:

- **Read:** opens the full review above the table, with the full text.
- **Approve:** publishes the review. You see "Review published."
- **Reject:** takes a new review off the queue. It never shows. You see "Review taken off the site."

Read also shows **Approve** and **Reject** buttons that ask you to confirm first ("Publish this review?" and "Take this review off the queue?"). The menu choices act at once.

### When a visitor changes a live review

The live review stays on the site while the change waits. The row says **Change waiting**, and the table shows the new text.

- **Approve** replaces the live text and stars with the new ones.
- **Reject** discards the change and keeps the live review. You see "Change discarded. The live review stays."

This holds while **Approve reviews before they show** is on. See [Settings](../settings/settings-reference.md).

## Published

Published lists the reviews visitors can see, newest first. In the **Decide** menu:

- **Read:** opens the full review.
- **Reply:** opens a box under the review. See below.
- **Delete:** takes the review off the site and out of the average. You see "Review taken off the site."

### Reply to a review

1. Choose **Reply**. The box is titled "Reply to" and the person's name.
2. Write your answer. It can be up to 1000 characters.
3. Choose **Save reply**. You see "Reply saved."

The reply shows under the review on your site, marked **Owner reply**, with its date. You can write one reply per review. To change it, choose **Reply** again and edit it. To remove it, clear the box and save. If the visitor later rewrites their review, your reply stays.

### Delete a guest's reviews by email

Use this for a privacy request from someone who gave you their email.

1. At the bottom of **Published**, open **Delete a guest's reviews by email**.
2. Type the address under **Guest email**.
3. Choose **Delete those reviews**.

You see "Deleted 2 reviews." or "No reviews with that email." This removes the reviews for good and updates the averages. The address must match exactly (capitals do not matter). It works only for reviews written while **Ask guests for an email** was on, because other reviews carry no address. It removes up to 100 reviews at a time. Run it again if there are more. A review with a mistyped address finds nothing.

## Two admins at once

If another admin decides a review while you have the screen open, your click may say "That review changed after this page loaded. Check it again." or "That review no longer exists." Reload the screen and look again.
