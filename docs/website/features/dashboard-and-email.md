# Dashboard card and email

**Included in Free.** WB Reviews has no paid version.

Two things tell you a review is waiting: a card on your admin dashboard, and an optional email.

## The dashboard card

The **Reviews waiting** card sits on the EmDash dashboard. It shows one number, **Waiting**, and an **Open Waiting** link that takes you to the Waiting screen. The number counts new reviews plus changes to live reviews that need a decision.

This card works on every site. It needs no email setup.

## The email

WB Reviews can send a short email when reviews are waiting.

- It goes out at most once an hour, on the hour, and only while something is waiting.
- It says reviews are waiting on your site (with the site name) and tells you to sign in and open Reviews, then Waiting.
- It has no review text and no link, so nothing private travels by email.
- It goes to the address you type under **Send the notice to** on the **Email** tab of Settings.
- It starts off. Turn on **Email me when reviews are waiting** and type the address in the same step.

### What the email needs

Your site must be able to send mail. EmDash has no mail sending of its own. Install an email plugin, such as the official Resend plugin, or no email goes out. The dashboard card works without one.

Use **Send a test notice** on the **Email** tab to check that the site can send email: it sends one notice to the saved address. If the site has no email provider, it says "No notice was sent: this site has no email provider." and the tab shows **This site has no email provider** until a test works. Saving the tab only saves ("Saved.") and sends nothing.

### Reviewers get no email

WB Reviews never emails the person who wrote a review. The only emails it sends are this notice and a test notice when you press Send a test notice, both to you.
