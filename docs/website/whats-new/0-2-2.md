# What's new in 0.2.2

**Included in Free.** WB Reviews has no paid version.

- **Updating the kit on a set-up site.** When your post page and post card already show WB Reviews, `scripts/add-to-site.sh` now says the lines are already in place instead of asking you to paste them again. A template that still needs them gets its paste lines as before.

Copy the site kit again after you update: run step 2 of the [install guide](../getting-started/install.md) with the new tag.
