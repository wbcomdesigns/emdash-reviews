# What's new in 0.3.2

**Included in Free.** WB Reviews has no paid version. No new permission. Copy the site kit again with tag `v0.3.2` (step 2 of the [install guide](../getting-started/install.md)): the review card, My reviews and the shared files changed.

- **Long links no longer break the page.** A review whose text, name or headline is one long word or link now wraps inside its card. Before, the whole post could scroll sideways on a phone.
- **Paragraphs stay paragraphs.** Line breaks and blank lines a reviewer types show on the page and in My reviews, and so do the ones in your reply.
- **Decide is always on screen.** On Waiting and Published the Decide button is the first column, so you can moderate from a phone. Long names and page titles are shortened in the table; open the review to read them in full.
- **Clearer reject message.** Rejecting a waiting review now says "Review rejected. It will not be shown."
- **Settings tells the truth about guests.** The plugin sees only your own visit to the admin. When that visit carries no visitor address, Settings says **This page cannot tell whether guests can review** and how to check (open a post signed out), instead of saying guests are blocked.
- **Rating joined to the article.** The rating in the page's structured data now belongs to the article EmDash already describes on the page. Search engines decide whether to show stars; Google does not show review stars for blog posts today.
- **Deleted pages take their reviews with them.** When a page is deleted for good (not just trashed), its reviews and rating are deleted too. A page with many reviews is cleared in steps every few minutes.
- **Faster lists on big sites.** New storage indexes for each list that filters and sorts. EmDash builds them the first time the plugin loads after the update; for that moment a write can be asked to try again.
