**Why is there no reviews block after install?**
The plugin cannot add pages or CSS. Copy the site kit components onto your templates, then reload.

**Can guests write a review?**
Yes, when Settings says anyone can review. A guest gives a name, and an email if you ask for one. Guest reviews always wait for approval.

**Do new reviews go live straight away?**
Only member reviews, and only if you turn off approval. With it on, which is the default, a review waits until you approve it. A change to a live review also waits; the published text stays until you approve the change.

**What is a star-only rating?**
Stars without text. It counts in the average and the bars, and it does not appear in the written list.

**Does it send email?**
At most one short notice an hour when reviews are waiting, and only if you turn that on and the site has mail (the official Resend plugin). The notice has no review text and no link.

**Is there a paid version?**
No. Everything here is free.
