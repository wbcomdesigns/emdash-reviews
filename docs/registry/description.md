WB Reviews adds star ratings and moderated reviews to any EmDash content: posts, pages, products or listings. Visitors read the average, the star breakdown and the written reviews. Members or guests leave one review per page. You approve what goes live.

## What you get

- **Ratings on any content.** Tick the site's own content types by name. Nothing is tied to posts.
- **Written reviews and stars alone.** Text is optional. A star-only rating counts in the average and does not appear in the list.
- **Members or anyone.** Guests give a name (and an email if you ask). Guest reviews always wait for approval. One review per visitor.
- **Moderation.** Waiting and Published pages, find by text, and delete a guest's reviews by email.
- **Owner reply.** One public reply under a review, written in admin.
- **Search engines.** The rating is added to the page as structured data.
- **A dashboard card** for what is waiting, plus an optional hourly email with no review text and no link.

## How it fits together

The plugin is the engine: routes, storage and the admin screens. An EmDash plugin cannot add pages to your site, so the public screens ship as a site kit you copy in once. See Installation.

Reviews are not core comments. Comments stay for conversation. This plugin adds a rating, one entry per person, an average, a bar breakdown and an owner reply.
